# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through a lightweight, iterative lifecycle. Teams begin by validating the business need, stakeholders, measurable success criteria, and proposed timeline. Approved initiatives move into planning, where the backlog is prioritized and estimated, risks and dependencies are recorded, acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done are established, and release milestones are agreed.

During execution, the team uses project boards to make work visible, follows disciplined pull-request and CI practices, conducts regular standups and demos, and reviews risks and blockers on a recurring cadence. Project Managers coordinate delivery and communication, Product Managers define outcomes and prioritize work, Developers implement and test features, and QA/Testing validates quality and acceptance.

Releases use pre-release checks, passing automated and security tests, smoke tests, rollback plans, deployment verification, and stakeholder communication. The lifecycle closes with retrospectives and follow-through on owned, time-bound improvement actions so lessons are converted into repeatable practice.
